## Bugs I Found & Fixed
- **Unverified Role Escalation**: Low-privilege viewers previously had access to trigger governance action approvals.
  - *Fix*: Bound `POST /v1/actions/:id/approve` strictly to `Role.NEXUS_OPERATOR` and `POST /v1/friday/command` to `Role.NEXUS_ADMIN`.
- **Public Key Ingestion Leak**: Public keys were previously evaluated in-memory without persistent site association.
  - *Fix*: Mapped keys to PostgreSQL `api_keys` with SHA-256 cryptographic hashing and site-level isolation.
