## Bugs I Found & Fixed
- **ToolBus Callable Await TypeError**: Synchronous lambdas registered on ToolBus failed when awaited as coroutines.
  - *Fix*: Added `inspect.iscoroutinefunction()` checks in `ToolBus.execute()` to handle both async and sync executors safely.
- **Uncontrolled Transient 5xx Retries**: Upstream AI gateway bursts caused hammering without exponential delays.
  - *Fix*: Implemented `asyncio.sleep(0.5 * (2 ** (attempt - 1)))` backoff schedule across 3 configured retry attempts.
- **Duplicate Tool Execution Under Burst Traffic**: Re-triggered agent loops caused redundant external side-effects.
  - *Fix*: Integrated Redis atomic `SET key locked NX EX 86400` within `ToolBus._check_and_set_idempotency()`.
