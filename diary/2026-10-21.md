## Key Decisions & Architecture
- Strict Tenant Isolation: All operations, sessions, visitors, DLQ messages, and attribution reports strictly enforce tenant_id.
- Statistical Rigor: CRO experiments require two-proportion z-tests (p < 0.05, z > 1.96) before winning variant rollout.
- Zero-Downtime Resilience: Canary controller instantly aborts and rolls back to 0% baseline if error rate > 1.0% or SLO breaches.
- Cryptographic Approvals: Closed-loop tool approvals are signed with HMAC-SHA256, guaranteeing tamper-proof execution.
